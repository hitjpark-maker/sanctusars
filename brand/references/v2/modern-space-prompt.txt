Create ONE new contemporary sacred fine-art painting, vertical 4:5. This is a modern work combining expressive painted human presence with LARGE QUIET COLOR FIELDS and deliberately incomplete space. Start a fresh painting, not a modification of a previous image. The spiritual subject is the Catholic Gospel line Luke 10:5, "Peace to this house": human disciples offering a household patient goodwill before knowing whether they will be welcomed. This is a pictorial interpretation, not a literal reconstruction of an ancient street.

PRIMARY VISUAL LANGUAGE: dramatic chiaroscuro with Rembrandt-like concentration of light, reinterpreted through a cool contemporary palette and radically edited space. Dominant fields of deep ink-blue, saturated dark violet, soft slate-blue and cold luminous off-white; small warm rose and muted coral passages in living skin. Do not give the picture a brown-and-gold, antique, amber-varnished appearance. Light is a selective silvery encounter on faces, hands and a few edges, not a theatrical beam. Skin is warmly alive despite the cool light.

Most of the support is covered in thin, MATTE, nearly smooth painted color. Two or three generous, asymmetrical shapes interlock, some with decisive edges and others fading into soft translucent veils. A few broad, freely drawn brush passages describe the humans. Strong contrast between spacious quiet paint and sensitively brushed living forms. No full-surface impasto, knife flakes, pebbled or fish-scale texture, crackle, patina or artificially aged surface. This is real-looking hand painting, not flat vector graphics, poster design, a collage or a photographic filter.

People are built in blended pigment throughout: curved brush drawing models cheeks, lids, lips and fingers. Complete readable eyes and mouths, coherent natural hands; selective edges and grouped hair rather than photographic pores and individual hairs. Faces are not pasted photographic fragments. Preserve a gentle, individual, attentive expression, never hollow or blank eyes, blue corpse skin, mannequin smiles or distorted anatomy. Omission belongs to architecture and clothing, never erased eyes or severed body parts. Keep arms connected through softly indicated sleeves even where the clothing merges with a color field.

The observer should sense peace through the relationship between people, the unhurried gaze and a pause between gestures. No demanding hand at the lens, giant foreground anatomy, stock advertising smile, wings, halos or decorative heavenly effects. Leave parts of perspective unresolved so space is suggested through color rather than explained by walls, floor tiles or furnishings. Do not simply show a realistic biblical scene behind geometric decoration: the chromatic arrangement IS the space of the encounter. Standalone artwork, no text, lettering, signature, logo, frame or copy area.

WIDE COMPOSITION: A THRESHOLD REMEMBERED THROUGH LIGHT. Compose a nearly abstract interior out of a vast ink-blue curved shadow on the left, an uneven tall cold-white shape offset to the right, and a low violet/slate passage. The white shape evokes an opening but has no complete frame, lintel, masonry or literal rectangular door. Its lower edge unexpectedly stops and continues as a pale translucent color plane at a different spatial depth; the viewer completes the missing space. At the lower right, a resident turning toward two modestly small visiting disciples becomes visible only in softly painted face, upper body and a quiet hand near the chest. The two visitors are lower left of the resident, one gently inclining his head to speak and the second waiting behind. Their eyes and body connections remain readable; they are ordinary humans and not ghosts. A thin reflected cool light touches the three people and bridges only a fragment of the threshold. No glowing bodies, fantasy beams, floating figures, or impossible joints. Their clothes merge into the surrounding color masses before reaching the bottom, so no tiled floor or line of sandaled feet explains the scene. The encounter is small within a monumental, asymmetrical field of quiet color. Let respectful distance and the resident attentive turn make the greeting of peace legible. Modern spatial ambiguity, very little architecture, no tree, landscape, village, props or antique-brown palette.
